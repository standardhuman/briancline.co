SailorSkills Pro

The operations app for hull cleaning and dive service businesses. Log the job at the dock in about a minute, and that one entry becomes the owner's report, the invoice, the service history and the payroll record.

I use it every day to run my own hull-cleaning business. It schedules and routes the day, saves logs with no signal, and bills the card on file the same day. You can also delegate boats to other trusted divers: the boat lands on their schedule, they log the visit in the same app, and it goes onto payroll at the agreed share.

Free for up to 15 boats. Pro from $49 a month.

Rio

A holistic approach to training, nutrition and body. Workouts on iPhone and Apple Watch, meal planning, food and what it costs, and Apple Health data, with a coach that sees the whole picture.

A personal build. It isn't on the App Store, and the data is stored on a server I run at home.

Training

In use

Log sets on iPhone or Apple Watch. A push/pull/legs program with automatic progression, a plate calculator, supersets and a rest timer. The coach reviews every session and adjusts the loads.

Progress

In testing

A chart for every lift, estimated one-rep max and personal-record detection, built from the logged sets.

Meal planning

In use

The coach plans meals from what's in the kitchen and what's left of the day's protein and calories, writes prep checklists, and builds shopping lists that skip what's on hand, comparing prices by protein per dollar.

Food log and macros

In use

Log a meal from a photo of a label or a plate, or in a sentence. Calories and protein track against daily targets, and every food carries its cost per serving.

Health data

In use

A companion app syncs Apple Health (sleep, heart rate, weight, workouts) to the home server. Rio saves each workout back to Apple Health.

Daily coaching

In testing

Morning and evening cards in the app (the day's workout, meals and prep) and an Ask Rio chat, from a coach built on Claude. A weekly check-in is next.

In use: part of my routine, mostly through chatting with the coach. In testing: built and being tried out, including the app's own screens for it.

Background

For nearly 20 years I've taught sailing on San Francisco Bay. Docking, offshore passage making, everything in between. The part that lights me up is watching someone go from white-knuckled to confident in the space of an afternoon. Breaking complex skills into steps someone can actually use.

I build the same way. A marketplace that connects boat owners with trusted service providers. A guided practice for working through conflict with compassion. A mobile app that turns a 60-second voice memo into a professional invoice. Different tools, same job: make the hard thing approachable.

I'm also drawn to long distances. Solo sailing to Hawaii. A thousand miles on the Appalachian Trail. Cross-country motorcycling. Long mountain bike rides through Marin and Southern Utah. Sustained effort over time strips away everything that doesn't matter.
